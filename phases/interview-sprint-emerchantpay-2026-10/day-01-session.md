**Date:** 2026-10-06

**Status:** In progress

## Purpose

This session establishes the starting baseline for the seven-day emerchantpay Data Analyst interview sprint.

It is not a pass/fail test. The purpose is to identify what can already be done independently and where teaching should begin. No skill is treated as demonstrated until it is completed independently.

## What we are covering in this session

The baseline will assess:

- basic payment-rate calculations;
- weighted and combined rates;
- percentage-point versus relative percentage change;
- payment attempts versus distinct payments;
- basic payments terminology;
- basic SQL understanding;
- basic Excel formula use;
- business reasoning about why an overall approval rate can change.

After the diagnostic is graded, teaching begins at the earliest missing prerequisite.

## Baseline rules

Allowed:

- calculator;
- blank Excel workbook.

Not allowed during the first attempt:

- AI answers;
- tutorials;
- web searches;
- worked examples;
- answer keys.

"I don't know yet" is an acceptable answer.

For each answer, distinguish:

- Independent
- Assisted
- Unknown
